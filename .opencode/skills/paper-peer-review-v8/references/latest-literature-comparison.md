# Latest Relevant Literature and Comparator Audit

## Search intent

Find the strongest literature that could materially change novelty interpretation, comparator choice, or the meaning of the results.

Priority:

1. direct same-problem/same-benchmark/same-metric work from the last year;
2. highly relevant work from the last three years;
3. older foundational work only when needed.

For fairness, record whether each work was available before manuscript submission.

## Distinguish citation from comparison

For each candidate record:

- bibliographic identity;
- relevance;
- available before submission?;
- cited in manuscript?;
- experimentally compared?;
- comparison protocol fairness;
- whether omission affects novelty or performance claims.

A cited method is not an experimental comparator unless implemented/evaluated under reasonably comparable conditions.

## Formal top-three rule

The Word report shows only the three most important works, ranked by scientific relevance/decision impact. Full candidate records may remain in JSON.

## Full-text acquisition

Every work shown in the formal top-three audit must enter the lawful full-text acquisition workflow. Follow `literature-fulltext-acquisition.md`.

## Venue, dates and expanded requests

Apply M16 in [power-system-review-memory.md](power-system-review-memory.md). The formal table includes journal/conference/preprint venue, year/version, relevance, citation status and experimental-comparison status. Unknown submission timing is `null`. When the user requests 3–5 related papers, add a separate expanded download list, not extra formal audit rows. Every explicitly requested download item receives a verified lawful PDF or access-status record; label landing pages separately from direct PDF links. Do not rank internal formulations without reading the relevant full text.
